Deep Cuts Support

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Deep Cuts is a music trivia app for iPhone with a daily ten-question mix, Name That Tune, cover songs, music stories and On This Day.

Found a wrong answer?

Tap Report a problem on the answer card. The report includes the question, so you only need to say what is wrong. Corrections reach the app without waiting for an update.

A song preview will not play

Previews stream from Apple Music, so they need a connection. Check your volume and connection, then try again. Apple sometimes moves a preview; the app looks up a fresh link on its own.

Changing your crates

Open the You tab to change which crates feed your daily ten.

Suggest a change

Use Suggest a change on the Today screen or in the You tab. Replies show up under You, Suggestions.
